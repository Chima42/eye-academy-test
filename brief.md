# Voice viva take-home

Candidate brief · Contract Developer · Four hours, unpaid

Build a short ophthalmology practice viva from an empty repository using TanStack Start, Firebase Auth and Firestore. Eye Academy will provide one fictional case and a glaucoma reference note (PDF), written by Eye Academy, to use as the reference source.

## What to build

- **Voice viva and interface.** A signed-in trainee can ask for case findings aloud. Read the finding back and display it on screen, preserving the correct eye, value and units. Say when a finding is unavailable. Ask one spoken follow-up that responds to what the trainee said. Make it clear when they can speak and when the examiner is responding.
- **Saved attempts.** After a refresh, the trainee can reopen their own transcript and revealed findings. Attempts are private to their owner.
- **Revision point from the reference note.** After the viva, retrieve relevant passages from the reference note and use them to generate one revision point based on the trainee's answer. Show the page number and a short supporting excerpt. If you can't find support in the note, tell the trainee. You may preprocess the PDF before the demo.

## What to submit

- **Repository and run instructions.** A local demo is fine. Stop after four hours and tell us what works and where you stopped.
- **Model choice and responsiveness.** Explain why you chose your voice model and name one alternative you considered. Measure a voice turn and explain when you started and stopped the timer.
- **Three repeatable checks.** Cover an available finding, an unavailable finding and whether a citation from the note supports the revision point. Include expected and observed results. For the citation check, record which pages were retrieved and which passage you cited. Manual checks are fine.
- **Short handover.** Explain how you prepared the reference note and selected relevant passages. AI coding tools are welcome. Give one concrete example of work they helped you with and how you verified it.

If time remains, add or sketch one improvement of your choice and explain why you prioritised it.

In the walkthrough, we'll introduce a small change and ask you to check what happens. You can use your usual AI tools.
